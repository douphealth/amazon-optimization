FAQs

What is the ideal protein percentage for French Bulldogs in 2026?

The ideal protein percentage on a dry matter basis (DMB) is 33–36%. This level supports lean muscle growth without placing excessive strain on the kidneys, which is critical for brachycephalic breeds.

Can I feed my Frenchie a raw diet safely?

Yes, but transition slowly over 7–10 days and consult your vet. Use single-protein sources like duck or cattle and add collagen toppers. Monitor stool quality and weight weekly.

How do I handle food allergies in French Bulldogs?

Start with an 8-week hydrolyzed protein elimination diet, then introduce novel proteins like insect-based larvae. Track skin scores daily and rotate proteins every 4–6 weeks.

What supplements are essential for muscle and joint health?

Green-lipped mussel powder, wild salmon oil, and soil-based probiotics are core. Total monthly cost is under $11. Avoid generic multivitamins and Chinese collagen chews.

How many calories should my French Bulldog eat daily?

Feed 20–25 kcal per pound of ideal body weight, not current weight. Use a BCS chart to determine ideal weight. Overfeeding is the #1 cause of obesity in Frenchies.

What are the emergency red flags for diet-related issues?

Post-meal retching >5 seconds, urine pH >7.5, and black tarry stools are emergencies. These indicate megaesophagus, urinary alkalosis, or gastric ulcers requiring immediate veterinary care.

How often should I rotate protein sources?

Rotate at least one major protein source every 4–6 weeks. This prevents allergy buildup and palate fatigue. Use a rotating “Bingo Sheet” of proteins like duck, cattle, fish, and insect-based options.
