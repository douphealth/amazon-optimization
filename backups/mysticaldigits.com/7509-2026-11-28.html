Common Mistakes That Kill Your Accuracy

I've made every mistake possible. Here are the 3 that cost me months of progress.

Mistake #1: Rolling Too Often

Asking the same question daily is like poking a wound. It won't heal. I checked my logs: when I rolled the same question within 48 hours, answers got confusing 89% of the time.

Rule: One question, one roll, wait 7 days minimum before re-asking. The universe needs time to move.

Mistake #2: Ignoring "No Answer" Rolls

Sometimes dice show nonsense. Like three conflicting symbols that make no sense. I used to force an interpretation. Wrong.

"No answer" means:

  • Question is too vague
  • Timing is wrong (ask later)
  • You're not ready for the answer

When this happens, stop. Wait 3 days. Try again with a more specific question.

Mistake #3: Confirmation Bias

This is the silent killer. You want a specific answer, so you interpret dice to fit your desire.

Example: You want to quit your job. Roll: Taurus/Venus/2nd (money, stability). You interpret as "stay for the money." But maybe it means "find beauty in your current work."

Fix: Ask someone else to interpret your rolls. I did this with 20 readings. External interpreters matched mine only 55% of the time. That's a problem. It means we see what we want.

⚠️ Warning

Never roll when angry, drunk, or desperate. I rolled at 2am after a breakup once. Got Scorpio/Pluto/12th: "You're drowning in obsession." Accurate, but useless. Your emotional state contaminates the reading. Wait until you're neutral.
