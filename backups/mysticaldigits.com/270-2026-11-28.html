Frequently Asked Questions

Is karmic debt punishment?

Nope. It’s an unpaid internship you volunteered for. The faster you finish the coursework, the quicker you level up.

Can I have all four debts?

Technically yes—I’ve seen it twice in 12 years—but it’s extremely rare. If the calculator flags three or four, focus on the one in your Expression first; that’s the loudest.

Do I need my birth time?

No. Karmic debt is tied to the name you were given at birth and your birthday, not the clock.

Does marriage change my karmic number?

Your birth name debt stays forever. However, adopting a new surname can add a secondary overlay. For that, run the calculator again with your married name and compare.

How often should I rerun the calculator?

Once is enough for the debt itself. Revisit yearly to track progress on the clearing templates above.
