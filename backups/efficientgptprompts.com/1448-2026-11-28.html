When writing self-discovery journal questions, it’s important to be specific and to focus on the things that are important to you. Try to avoid asking questions that are too broad or too general. Instead, ask questions that will help you to learn more about yourself and your goals.

Here are three examples of self-discovery journal questions:

* What are my biggest strengths and weaknesses?
* What are my goals for the future?
* What are the things that make me happy?

These are just a few examples of the many different types of self-discovery journal questions that you can ask yourself. The best questions are the ones that are relevant to your own life and that will help you to learn more about yourself.

Clear Prompt Instructions

To write a clear prompt about self-discovery journal questions, you need to:

* Be specific. Your prompt should focus on a specific topic or area of your life that you want to explore.
* Be relevant. Your prompt should be relevant to your own life and interests.
* Be open-ended. Your prompt should allow for a variety of responses.

Here are some tips for writing a clear prompt:

* Use active verbs.
* Avoid using too much jargon or technical language.
* Keep your prompt short and to the point.
