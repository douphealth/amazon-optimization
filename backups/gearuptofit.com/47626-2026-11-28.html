Frequently Asked Questions

Which calcium is best for women over 50?

Women over 50 need calcium citate because it absorb without normal stomach acid and does not cause constipation. Take 500 mg or less with food for better absorption.

Can calcium cause kidney stones?

Yes, too much calcium can cause kidney stones. Eat normal foods and drink water. Ask your doctor before taking calcium pills.

Is calcium carbonate safe daily?

Yes, calcium carbonate is safe daily if you keep it under 2,000 mg from food and supplements. Stay under this amount and you avoid kidney stones or constipation.

Do vegans need special calcium?

No. Vegans get calcium from the same place cows do: plants. Eat leafy greens, beans, and fortified drinks and you cover it.

When should I take calcium tablets?

Take calcium tablets with food, especially the evening meal, so stomach acid helps absorb it and you remember daily.

How much magnesium with calcium?

Take 1 part magnesium for every 2 parts calcium. Most adults need 100 mg magnesium and 200 mg calcium at the same meal, so the minerals work together and do not upset the stomach.

Are heavy metals in calcium?

Heavy metals are not part of calcium itself, but some calcium supplements may contain traces of heavy metals like lead or cadmium due to contamination in the sourcing. To avoid this, choose calcium supplements from reputable manufacturers that test for heavy metals and meet safety standards.

Is citrate better than carbonate?

Citrate absorbs easier than carbonate, especially if you have low stomach acid, but carbonate costs less and gives more calcium per pill. Pick citrate if your digestion is delicate; pick carbonate if you want the cheapest price.
