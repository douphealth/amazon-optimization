Clear Prompt Instructions

When writing an Interstellar Exploration Prompt, it is important to be clear and concise. You want to give your readers a clear idea of what the story is about, and what they are supposed to do.

Here are a few tips for writing a clear prompt:

* Start with a strong hook. Your prompt should grab the reader’s attention and make them want to know more.
* Be specific about the setting and characters. Where is the story taking place? Who are the main characters?
* Provide a clear goal for the characters to achieve. What do they want to accomplish?
* Leave room for creativity. Don’t give away too much information, or your readers will have no room to explore their own ideas.
