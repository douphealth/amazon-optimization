How fast should beginners increase speed or distance? +

Follow the 10% rule to avoid overuse injuries: do not increase your total weekly running distance or time by more than 10% from the previous week. If you ran for 100 minutes total in Week 1, aim for 110 minutes in Week 2. Similarly, increase speed in small increments (0.2-0.3 mph). Always keep at least 80% of your weekly mileage at an easy, conversational pace to allow tendons, ligaments, and your cardiovascular system to adapt safely.
