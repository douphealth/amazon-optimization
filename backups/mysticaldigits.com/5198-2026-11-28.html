How to Figure Out Your Life Number in Numerology (Step-by-Step)

  1. Add every digit of your birth date (MM/DD/YYYY) separately.
  2. Reduce each section to a single digit or Master number (11, 22, 33).
  3. Now add the three results together; reduce again.
  4. That final digit is your Life Path number.

Example: July 19, 1989 → 7 + (1+9) + (1+9+8+9) = 7 + 1 + 27 = 7 + 1 + 9 = 17 → 1 + 7 = 8
