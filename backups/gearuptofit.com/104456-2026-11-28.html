Technology Deep Dive: What Actually Changed

The Vaporfly 4 isn't a revolution — it's Nike sanding down everything that made the Vaporfly 3 great until only speed remained. Four changes matter:

🪶 Lighter ZoomX Foam

Nike reformulated its most responsive foam to weigh less while returning more energy, with a slightly trimmed midsole profile. The result: the same bounce, less shoe.

📐 Steeper Carbon Flyplate

The full-length carbon fiber plate sits at a steeper, more aggressive angle than in the Vaporfly 3. Paired with the lower 6mm drop, it creates a stronger "teeter-totter" effect for a more propulsive feel at toe-off.

🧦 Stripped-Down Upper

Engineered mesh with a smaller heel and shorter tongue cuts weight and improves lockdown — and for the first time in the line, the lacing is centered (the offset-lace trademark is gone). Trade-off: the fit runs narrower than the 3 — wide-footed runners should try before buying.

🦶 Thinner Outsole + Stable Heel

Thinner rubber saves grams without giving up wet-surface traction. A redesigned heel adds a touch of stability, and an internal foam pod at the heel seam cuts friction.

Sizing & Fit Guide

⚠️ The one fit warning that matters: the Vaporfly 4 runs true to size in length but narrow in width — noticeably narrower than the Vaporfly 3. Runners with wide feet or high-volume feet consistently report a snug, sometimes uncomfortable fit. If that's you, try them on in-store before buying, or look at the roomier Vaporfly 3 on clearance instead. Everyone else: order your normal running shoe size.

  • Length: true to size — no need to size up or down
  • Width: narrow; not wide-foot friendly
  • Heel lockdown: improved vs the 3, thanks to the redesigned heel and internal foam pod
  • Tongue: shorter and lighter than the 3 — no ankle digging
  • Socks: thin race-day socks work best; the engineered mesh is breathable

Nike Vaporfly 4: Frequently Asked Questions

When did the Nike Vaporfly 4 come out?

The Nike Vaporfly 4 launched on March 1, 2025 — so it has over a year and a half of real-world race results behind it, unlike brand-new releases.

How much does the Nike Vaporfly 4 cost?

It launched at $260 and is currently listed at $270 on Nike.com. That's $5 less than the Nike Alphafly 4 ($275) — and third-party retailers frequently discount it further.

How much does the Nike Vaporfly 4 weigh?

~190 grams in a men's US 10 and 152 grams in a women's US 8 — the lightest Vaporfly Nike has ever made.

What's the difference between the Vaporfly 4 and the Vaporfly 3?

The 4 is lighter in the upper, midsole, and outsole, with a steeper carbon Flyplate angle, a lower 6mm drop (35mm/29mm stack), reformulated lighter ZoomX foam, and a thinner outsole. The trade-off: a narrower fit than the roomier 3.

Vaporfly 4 vs Alphafly 4: which should I buy?

5K–half marathon or snappy ride → Vaporfly 4 (190g, $270). Marathon or max cushion → Alphafly 4 (208g, $275, Air Zoom pods). The price gap is only $5 at retail — decide on ride feel, or buy whichever is on sale.

Is the Nike Vaporfly 4 good for beginners?

No. It's a carbon-plated race-day shoe that changes your running mechanics. Beginners should build base mileage in a supportive daily trainer first.

Is the Nike Vaporfly 4 true to size?

Yes in length, but narrow in width. Order your normal size unless you have wide feet — wide-footed runners consistently find the Vaporfly 4 too snug and should try it on first.

How many miles do Nike Vaporfly 4 last?

Nike doesn't publish a figure. Like most carbon-plated super shoes, expect roughly 150–200 miles of peak performance. Save them for races and key workouts to maximize lifespan.

Is the Nike Vaporfly 4 legal for races?

Yes. The 35mm heel stack is well under the 40mm World Athletics limit for road racing shoes.

Where can I buy the Nike Vaporfly 4?

On Nike.com, Amazon, and at running specialty retailers like Fleet Feet and Marathon Sports — in men's and women's versions and multiple colorways.

Should I buy the Vaporfly 4 or wait?

There's nothing to wait for — it's been out since March 2025, which means deals are already here. The only reason to wait: Nike left the Vaporfly out of its new Race-Day System of Speed, so a successor's timing is unknown. Buy on sale with confidence.

Is the Nike Vaporfly 4 worth it?

For racers from the mile to the marathon who want Nike's lightest, snappiest ride: yes — especially discounted. For marathon-first runners wanting maximum cushioning: consider the Alphafly 4 instead. It's not a daily trainer.

Can I run a full marathon in the Nike Vaporfly 4?

Yes, but it's not the ideal marathon shoe for everyone. Lighter, efficient runners race marathons in it successfully — Brigid Kosgei won the 2026 Tokyo Marathon in a 2:14:29 course record wearing the Vaporfly 4. But the lower, firmer stack beats up heavier runners' legs over 26.2 miles, and most expert testers cap its sweet spot at the half marathon. For a dedicated marathon shoe, see the Alphafly 4.

Does the Nike Vaporfly 4 have a carbon plate?

Yes. It features a full-length carbon fiber Flyplate encased in ZoomX foam. For the 4, Nike steepened the plate angle for a more aggressive "teeter-totter" propulsion effect at toe-off.

Why isn't the Vaporfly 4 in Nike's Race-Day System of Speed?

Nike's 2026 race-day lineup (Apex, Alphafly 4, Swooshfly) omits the Vaporfly — but Nike has not discontinued it and still sells it at $270, describing the lineup as "a choice rather than a hierarchy." A successor's timing is unknown, which is another reason buying the proven 4 on sale makes sense.

Should I wait for the Nike Vaporfly 5?

There's no announced Vaporfly 5 or release date as of October 2026. Given the Vaporfly 4's uncertain place in Nike's future lineup, waiting is a gamble — and the 4 is already frequently discounted $50–100 below retail. If you need a racer now, buy the 4 on sale with confidence.
