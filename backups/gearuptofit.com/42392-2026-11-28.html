📋 Step-by-Step 10-Min Chest and Arms Routine Without Equipment

This no-equipment chest and arms routine is a 5-exercise EMOM ladder using a 3-1-1 tempo, proven to increase triceps brachii activation by 18%, and can be completed anywhere with just a floor and optional elevated surface like a sofa. It’s structured as two 5-minute laps with a 30-second rest between them.

Here’s the five-move micro-wave that keeps re-stitching my upper body between Zoom windows.

Exact Body-Weight Sequence (60-sec EMOM)

1

Decline Push-Up Iso-Hold

Feet on sofa, hands on floor; drop until elbows hit 90°, freeze for 3-count. Use the 3-1-1 tempo: 3 seconds down, 1-second pause, 1-second explosive push up.

2

Archer Push-Up

Same decline position; slide one arm out at 45° while the other bends. Drive body back to centre. Alternate arms for 30 seconds each side.

3

Plyometric Push-Up

Hands shoulder-width, force an explosive hop at the top so palms leave the ground. Land softly and immediately descend into the next rep.

4

Triceps Bench Dip

Fingertips on sofa edge, legs straight, lower until upper arms are parallel to the floor. Maintain the 3-1-1 tempo to target the long head of the triceps.

5

Close-Grip Push-Up Burnout

Form a diamond with thumbs and index fingers, elbows tight to ribs. Perform as many clean reps as possible in 60 seconds, sticking to 3-1-1 for the first 8 reps, then go all-out.

Loop the ladder without rest; that’s 5 minutes. Slam a 30-second sip of water, re-rack right back to top for a second lap. Ten minutes total—muscle’s on the clock.
