Clear Prompt Instructions

When crafting a Space Opera Drama Prompt, it is important to be clear and concise in your instructions. Here are a few tips for writing effective prompt instructions:

* Use active voice. Active voice is more engaging than passive voice. For example, “A group of rebels fight against an oppressive empire” is more engaging than “An oppressive empire is fought against by a group of rebels.”
* Be specific. Vague prompts are less likely to generate interesting stories. For example, “Write a story about a group of rebels” is less specific than “Write a story about a group of rebels who are fighting against an oppressive empire.”
* Leave room for open-ended responses. Don’t be afraid to let your readers’ imaginations run wild. The best prompts are those that allow for multiple interpretations and interpretations.

* Maintain plot coherence. Your plot should be clear and easy to follow. Make sure that the events of your story flow logically and that there are no major plot holes.
