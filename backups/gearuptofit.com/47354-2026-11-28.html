Frequently Asked Questions

How long before I feel creatine working?

Most people notice creatine’s effects within 7 days: muscles feel a bit fuller and the last 1-2 reps in a set get easier. If you skip the 20 g-per-day loading phase, expect the same benefits after about 3–4 weeks of taking 3–5 g daily.

Can I take creatine on an empty stomach?

Yes, you can take creatine on an empty stomach; it still absorbs well and works the same. Some people feel mild nausea when they do this, so if your stomach is sensitive, have it with a small snack or mix it into a shake.

Is creatine safe for kidneys in healthy men?

Yes. In healthy men, dozens of long-term studies up to 2025 show that taking the usual 3–5 g of creatine a day does not harm the kidneys; blood tests for creatinine stay normal and kidney function is unchanged.

Should I use creatine before or after lifting?

Take creatine after your workout; a 2025 review shows muscles soak up more of it when they’re already pumped. Stir 5 g into your post-lift protein shake and you’re covered for the day.

Does caffeine block creatine absorption?

No, caffeine does not block creatine absorption; the two are safe to take together. Early, small studies once hinted at a clash, but larger 2023-2024 trials show they do not reduce each other’s benefits when used in normal daily amounts.

Can women use the same creatine as men?

Yes—plain creatine monohydrate is the same molecule for everyone, so women can scoop from the same tub as men; no “for her” blend is needed. Pick a brand that shows third-party testing for purity, start with 3–5 g a day, and drink an extra glass of water to keep muscles hydrated.

What happens if I stop taking creatine?

Your muscles slowly drop the extra water they hold, so your weight may dip a few pounds. Strength and performance stay the same if you keep training and eating enough protein. Any belly upset or bloating you had should fade within a week.

Is flavored creatine higher in calories?

Plain creatine has zero calories; flavored powders only add 5–15 calories per scoop from a little sugar or natural flavor, so the difference is tiny unless you take several servings a day.
