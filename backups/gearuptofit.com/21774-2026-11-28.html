How Do I Make Homemade Almond Butter Without Preservatives?

You can make the best almond butter at home in 15 minutes. No preservatives. Just almonds and a food processor. It’s cheaper, fresher, and tastier than store-bought. You control what goes in your jar.

Simple Steps for Fresh Almond Butter

Start with 2 cups of raw almonds. No skins, no salt. Roast them at 350°F for 10 minutes until warm and fragrant. Cool for 2 minutes. Heat helps release natural oils faster.

Add almonds to your food processor. Blender won’t work. Process for 10–15 minutes. Scrape down sides every few minutes. The mix goes from chopped, to meal, to clumpy, then smooth like butter. It’s done when creamy and golden.

Optional: add one drop of vanilla or pinch of cinnamon. Don’t add oil. Almonds have enough. Store in a glass jar in the fridge for up to 3 weeks. Stir well before each use. Separation is normal.

Making nut butters at home cuts waste and preservatives. A 2025 study found 78% of people who made their own almond butter saw improved taste satisfaction compared to brands [1]. Try it. You’ll never go back to preservative-laden jars.
