What are the five proper practices (Niyamas) of yoga?

The five Niyamas are personal observances: Saucha (cleanliness), Santosha (contentment), Tapas (discipline), Svadhyaya (self-study), and Ishvara Pranidhana (surrender). For a modern practitioner, this means a clean mat (Saucha), celebrating small wins (Santosha), showing up consistently (Tapas), reviewing your practice videos (Svadhyaya), and not forcing outcomes (Ishvara Pranidhana).
