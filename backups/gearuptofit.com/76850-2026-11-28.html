Suunto Race Titanium Altitude

If you like climbing mountains or hiking up tall hills, this watch has a special feature. It has an altimeter that measures how high up you are. The altimeter can determine your precise altitude anywhere on Earth. It works whether you are at the bottom of the ocean or the top of Mount Everest.

The altimeter works by sensing tiny changes in air pressure. As you go up, the air gets thinner, and the watch can detect that. It’s more precise than just using GPS. You’ll always know the exact altitude you’ve reached and how much further you need to go to reach the summit.
