Finding Your Shadow Numbers in 10 Minutes

  1. Write your full birth name—no middle-name shortcuts.
  2. Assign each letter its Pythagorean value (A=1, B=2 … I=9, repeat 1–9).
  3. Circle every digit that appears. The digits 1–9 NOT circled are your shadow numbers.
  4. If only one digit is missing, double it; you’ll work on two shadows.

FAQs

Can I change my shadow numbers by changing my name?

A legal name change adds new vibrations but never erases the original blueprint. Think of it as installing extra RAM, not a new operating system.

What if I have no missing digits?

You still have a shadow—look at the digit that appears least often or examine master numbers that are challenging you right now. Complacency loves the “perfect chart” illusion.

Is shadow work dangerous?

It stirs repressed emotion. If you’re in active trauma recovery, pair the ritual with a licensed therapist. Used responsibly, it’s safer than ignoring the patterns.

How is this different from tarot shadow work?

Numerology gives you a fixed numeric map, so progress is measurable. Tarot tends to fluctuate with each shuffle; both tools complement but don’t replace one another.

Do I have to use candles?

No. Sound baths, mudras, or affirmations work—pick the sensory key that unlocks the emotion fastest for you.
