Forget vanity milestones. I track four KPIs with clients:

FAQ

  1. Can numerology replace therapy?
    No. It’s a mirror, not a cure. Use it alongside professional help for clinical issues.
  2. Is the system different in other languages?
    Standard Western numerology uses the 1-9 alphabet; Chaldean and Hindi systems tweak values. Stick to one system per chart to avoid noise.
  3. How often should I revisit my numbers?
    Core numbers: once—they never change. Personal Year: every birthday. Karmic lessons: review quarterly.
  4. Do nicknames or married names matter?
    They add “overlay” energy. Keep birth names for soul purpose; use current names for daily vibe tweaks.
