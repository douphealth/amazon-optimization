Life Path compatibility compares symbolic numerology themes; it cannot reliably assess two people or predict relationship outcomes. Calculate both dates using the same stated method, then use the themes—if useful—as conversation prompts, not scores or labels. Real communication, choices, and experience matter more than a number.

Calculate each date separately

This guide reduces month, day, and year separately, preserving 11, 22, or 33 when reached; it then adds those results and reduces any total that is not a single digit or master number. Numerology.com describes this calculation approach, as does World Numerology.[1][3] Numerology.com separately says its compatibility readings use single digits, so it maps 11→2, 22→4, and 33→6. That reading convention is distinct from preserving master numbers during calculation; conventions vary.[1]

  • May 3, 1998: month 5; day 3; year 1+9+9+8=27→9. Sum: 5+3+9=17→8. Result: 17/8 (final Life Path 8; intermediate total 17).
  • December 11, 1990: month 12→3; day 11 stays 11; year 1+9+9+0=19→10→1. Sum: 3+11+1=15→6. Result: 6.

Tradition versus evidence

Numerology publications describe symbolic meanings and compatibility interpretations; some publish preferred pairings.[1] These sources document numerology traditions, not evidence that birth dates determine personality, attraction, or relationship outcomes. The matrix avoids assigning scores, favorable or unfavorable labels, or predictions. Relationship decisions should rest on lived experience, consent, communication, and circumstances—not a number.

Frequently asked questions

Are some Life Path pairings incompatible?

This guide labels no pair incompatible. Published numerology pairings are symbolic conventions, not dependable assessments of individuals.[1]

Should 11, 22, or 33 be reduced?

In the calculation demonstrated here, retain a master number when reached in a component; reduce a non-master total. Numerology.com then reduces 11, 22, and 33 to 2, 4, and 6 specifically for its compatibility convention. These are distinct steps, and traditions may differ.[1][3]

Can birth dates say whether a relationship will last?

No. A number comparison cannot establish or predict relationship outcomes.
