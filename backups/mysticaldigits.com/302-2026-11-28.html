Five Proven Steps to Pay Off 16

  1. Radical Honesty Audit – List every area where you bend truth to look better. Start with your resume and relationship status. Publish it to one trusted friend.
  2. Strip the Resume – Downsize lifestyle faster than the universe can downsize it for you. Cancel the leased status car, archive thirst-trap feeds, move to modest housing.
  3. Ego Check Ritual – Each sunrise ask: “If nobody knew I did this, would I still do it?” If the answer is no, stop.
  4. Humility Service – Mentor someone who can never repay you. The 16 energy dissolves when you give without audience.
  5. Solitude Rebuild – Schedule one full day a week alone, no technology. Journal what remains when applause is absent.
