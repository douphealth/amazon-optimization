Quick answer: Do not water a pothos on a fixed schedule. University of Maryland Extension recommends watering when the potting mix has dried at about two inches deep, and NC State Extension says pothos should have its well-drained mix dry out between waterings. When you do water, soak it until water runs from the drainage holes and empty the saucer.

How to tell when a pothos needs water

  • Finger test: push a finger about two inches into the mix. If it is dry, the plant probably needs water (Maryland Extension).
  • Lift test: lift the pot, pot and all. A pot with dry mix weighs much less than one with plenty of water in it (Maryland Extension).
  • Penetration: if your finger cannot go two inches deep, the mix may be too dense or the plant may be root-bound (Maryland Extension).

Pothos is not a plant that wilts to tell you it is thirsty, so check the pot rather than waiting for the leaves to droop, and remember that drooping can also come from too much water.

How to water

  1. Water from the top until water drains freely from the bottom of the container.
  2. Let it finish draining, then dump any excess from the saucer. Never let the pot sit in water (Maryland Extension; UF/IFAS).
  3. Wait until the mix has dried at the two-inch depth before watering again.

Maryland Extension also describes bottom watering: put water in the saucer, let the mix absorb it, then empty what is left. Either way, the pot must not stand in water afterwards.

What changes how often it needs water

Maryland Extension lists the potting medium, humidity and temperature among the factors that affect how often a plant needs water, which is why a schedule fails. The size of the pot and how much light the plant gets also change how fast the mix dries, so a pot in a dim room can stay wet far longer than the same plant by a bright window. UF/IFAS puts it simply: water when the soil is dry to the touch, and expect that to be less often than you think.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I water pothos indoors?

There is no number that fits every home. Check the pot every few days and water when the mix is dry about two inches down or the pot feels light.

Can a pothos sit in water?

Cuttings root easily in water (NC State), but a potted plant should not stand in water. Maryland Extension says never to let houseplants sit in water.

Is tap water okay?

Maryland Extension says softened water contains more dissolved minerals and should be avoided for houseplants.

Should I mist my pothos?

Maryland Extension says it is questionable whether misting really raises humidity. If you want to raise humidity, it suggests a pebble tray, grouping plants, or a humidifier.
