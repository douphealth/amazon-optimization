Common Mistakes That Derail Progress

I've seen these mistakes hundreds of times. Don't make them.

Mistake #1: Using the wrong date format. American format is MM/DD/YYYY. European is DD/MM/YYYY. Pick one and stick to it. Mixing them gives wrong results. I once calculated someone's Life Path as 3 when it was 7 because I used the wrong format. They didn't speak to me for a week.

Mistake #2: Forgetting to reduce master numbers in intermediate steps. 11/29/1991: the 11 reduces to 2 for the calculation. The 29 reduces to 11, which reduces to 2. So you're adding 2+2+... not 11+29+...

Mistake #3: Getting emotional about your numbers. If you're a 4 and want to be a 5, that's fine. Your Life Path isn't a prison—it's a starting point. You can develop any energy you want. The numbers just show your default settings.

Mistake #4: Using numerology to make decisions FOR you. Numbers inform, they don't decide. If your Personal Year is 9 and you want to start a business, start it. Just know you're swimming upstream. Bring a bigger paddle.

Mistake #5: Teaching before you're ready. If you can't calculate 10 birth dates perfectly, you're not ready to teach. Teaching bad information spreads confusion. Get your own house in order first.

Mistake #6: Ignoring the math. Numerology IS math. If your calculations are wrong, everything is wrong. I don't care if your "intuition" says 11/29/1954 is a 3. The math says 5. Trust the math.

Mistake #7: Making it your identity. "I'm a 7" is a description, not a definition. You're a complex human being. Numerology is one lens, not the entire picture. Don't let it limit you.

Numerology is accurate at calculating patterns and providing frameworks for interpretation. It's not fortune-telling. However, accuracy depends on correct calculations and realistic interpretation.
