Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best cord for macrame plant hangers?

Cotton cord in 3-5mm thickness is ideal for macrame plant hangers. It’s strong yet flexible for knots, eco-friendly, and doesn’t stretch much. Avoid nylon—it’s slippery and hard for beginners. Jute adds rustic charm but frays faster. Choose based on your plant’s weight for secure holds.

What else can I put in a macrame plant hanger?

Beyond plants, try lanterns, fairy lights, or glass terrariums in macrame hangers. Small succulents in jars work too. For non-plant uses, hang candles or ornaments. Ensure weight balance to avoid sagging. It’s versatile for boho decor beyond greenery.

How long does a macrame plant hanger take to make?

Beginner designs take 30-60 minutes once knots are practiced. Detailed patterns may need 2 hours. Prep materials ahead to speed up. With repetition, you’ll finish quicker, turning it into a relaxing hobby for home enhancement.

Can I wash macrame plant hangers?

Yes, spot clean cotton ones with mild soap and water. Air dry to prevent shrinkage. Avoid machine washing—knots may loosen. For stained hangers, gentle handwashing preserves the handmade quality and extends life.

What plants work best in hanging macrame?

Trailing varieties like pothos, ivy, or spider plants thrive in macrame hangers. They cascade beautifully, filling space. Avoid heavy topiaries—opt for lightweight pots. Check spider plant care for easy options.

Where can I buy macrame supplies?

Craft stores, online like Etsy or Amazon, or local markets offer cotton cord and rings. Look for bundles in beginner kits. For eco options, check sustainable brands. Prices start low, making it accessible for DIY enthusiasts.

  • Cotton
  • Single twist or braided
