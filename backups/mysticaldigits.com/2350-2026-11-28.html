How to Calculate Your Life Path Number (and Confirm You’re a 35)

Birth date 17-Aug-1990? Add:

  1. August = 8
  2. Day 17 → 1 + 7 = 8
  3. Year 1 + 9 + 9 + 0 = 19 → 1 + 9 = 10 → 1 + 0 = 1
  4. Total: 8 + 8 + 1 = 17 → 1 + 7 = 8

Because the un-reduced sum before the final 8 was 17—not 35—you’re an 8 with 17 overlay, not a 35. To land on 35 you need the pre-reduction total to be 35. Try 29-May-1988:

  • May = 5
  • Day 29 → 2 + 9 = 11
  • Year 1 + 9 + 8 + 8 = 26 → 2 + 6 = 8
  • Total: 5 + 11 + 8 = 24 → 2 + 4 = 6 … still not 35.

See why most blogs skip this? The math is rare. That rarity is the first clue you’re dealing with a unique numerology signature.
