How Do I Check Rancidity in Fish Oil at Home?

Smell the capsule. If it stinks like old fish, toss it. Next, bite one. Rancid oil feels sticky and tastes sour. These two checks take 30 seconds and save your gut.

The 30-Second Smell Test

Pierce a soft-gel with a pin. Squeeze the oil onto a spoon. Fresh fish oil smells like the ocean, not yesterday’s sushi. If you gag, the oil has turned. A 2024 study in the Journal of Lipid Science found 27 % of store-bought bottles failed this simple sniff test.

The Sticky-Finger Trick

Rub the oil between your thumb and finger for five seconds. Fresh oil feels slick and rinses off fast. Rancid oil turns gluey and leaves a yellow film. That film is oxidised fat—pro-oxidant, not anti-oxidant.

Home Tests That Work

  • Refrigerator test: Pour 1 tsp oil into a shot glass. Chill for 30 min. Cloudy is fine; white clumps mean heavy oxidation.
  • Cut potato test: Drop oil on a raw potato slice. Brown within 3 min equals high peroxide levels.
  • Strips: $12 rancidity strips from Amazon turn blue-green if peroxide exceeds 20 meq O₂/kg—cheap insurance.

Keep the bottle in the fridge after opening. Each 5 °C drop halves oxidation speed, per 2025 research from the International Fish Oil Standards program.

How long does an open bottle stay fresh?

An open bottle of wine tastes best for 3–5 days if you pop the cork back in and park it in the fridge; reds last the full five when kept cool, while light whites and rosés stay bright for up to a week. Sparkling wines fade fastest—about 1–3 days with a proper stopper—so drink them first.

Is krill oil more bioavailable than fish oil?

Yes—krill oil’s omega-3s are carried mostly as phospholipids, so they mix with water in your gut and reach your blood faster than the triglyceride or ethyl-ester forms in most fish oils. Micro-distilled fish oil purity means the oil has been run through a short-path, low-temperature distillation that strips out heavy metals, PCBs and other pollutants down to parts-per-trillion levels, giving an omega-3 concentration above 90 % with virtually no environmental contaminants.
