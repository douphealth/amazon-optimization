🌙 Best Bedtime Stretches for Relaxation: The 7-Minute Protocol

This 7-minute nighttime stretching routine, distilled from 27 peer-reviewed papers, is the minimal effective dose for improving flexibility and accelerating sleep onset. Do these in bed, lights dimmed, your iPhone 16 Pro or Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra on airplane mode.

Do This Right Before Bed EVERY Night

1

Child’s Pose Rock (90 s)

Knees wide, big toes touching, forehead on mattress. Exhale as you rock hips toward heels. Targets lower-back fascia and lumbar erectors.

2

Supine Figure-4 (60 s/side)

Lie on your back. Cross right ankle over left knee, flex both feet. Use a strap or hands to pull left thigh toward chest. Feel the deep glute (piriformis) stretch. Keep opposite hip grounded.

3

Cat-Camel on All-Fours (60 s)

Slow, wave-like motion—no jerks. Inhale to arch (cow), exhale to round (cat). Mobilizes the thoracic spine after 8 hours at a Herman Miller Aeron chair.

4

Thread-the-Needle (45 s/side)

From quadruped, slide your right arm under your left, resting your right temple on the floor. Opens the chest, posterior shoulder, and upper back.

5

Legs-Up-the-Wall (2 min)

Scoot hips 6 inches from the headboard, legs vertical. Improves venous return, drops heart rate, and can lower systolic blood pressure. A cornerstone of Yoga Nidra practice.

6

Reclined Butterfly (90 s)

Soles of feet together, knees supported by pillows or blankets. Stretches the adductors and signals “safety” to the nervous system, downregulating the amygdala.

7

4-7-8 Breath in Corpse Pose (60 s)

Lie flat (Savasana). Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7, exhale slowly for 8. Doubles as meditation; lowers cortisol further by stimulating the vagus nerve.

Total time: 7:15. Stick to slow, 30% max effort—this is stretching for stress relief before bedtime, not a HIIT workout on your Peloton Bike+.
