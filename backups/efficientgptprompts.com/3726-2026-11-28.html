Clear Prompt Instructions

When writing a prompt about nonfiction, it is important to be clear and concise. Your prompt should give the writer a clear idea of what you are looking for. Here are some tips for writing a clear prompt:

* Use specific language. Avoid vague terms like “interesting” or “creative.” Instead, use specific language that describes the type of story you are looking for.
* Be concise. Your prompt should be short and to the point. A good rule of thumb is to keep your prompt to one or two sentences.
* Provide relevant details. If you are looking for a specific type of story, be sure to provide relevant details in your prompt. For example, if you are looking for a story about a person who has overcome adversity, you could mention that the person is a cancer survivor.

Tips for Crafting a Compelling Story

When writing a nonfiction story, there are a few things you can do to make it more compelling:

* Develop strong characters. The characters in your story should be complex and relatable. They should have clear goals and motivations, and they should face challenges that make them grow and change.
* Maintain plot coherence. Your story should have a clear beginning, middle, and end. The events in your story should flow logically, and the characters should make decisions that are consistent with their personalities.
* Incorporate historical details. If your story is based on real events, be sure to incorporate accurate historical details. This will help to ground your story in reality and make it more believable.
* Experiment with different genres or tones. Nonfiction can be written in a variety of genres and tones. You can write a personal essay, a historical narrative, a memoir, or a journalistic article. You can also write your story in a serious or humorous tone. Experimenting with different genres and tones can help you find the best way to tell your story.
