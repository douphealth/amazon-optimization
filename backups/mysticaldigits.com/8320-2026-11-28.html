Pyramid Numerology Chart: Step-by-Step

  1. Write the full birth date DD-MM-YYYY in a horizontal line.
  2. Reduce each section to a single digit (except masters 11/22).
  3. Stack the four digits into one vertical “pyramid.”
  4. Connect the dots freehand; the outline reveals base energy.

Example: 14-07-1990 → 5 – 7 – 1 → pyramid sides of 5, 7, 1.

A pentagon (5) base with a heptagon (7) cap? Expect sudden travel for research.
