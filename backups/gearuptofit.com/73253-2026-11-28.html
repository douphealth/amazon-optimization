What makes the Polar Grit X Pro stand out as a rugged watch for multisport activities?

Is there a difference in design between the Polar Grit X Pro and the Grit X Pro Titan?

Can I customize my Polar Grit X Pro with different straps?

Absolutely, the Polar Grit X Pro Premium offers interchangeable strap options to suit both comfort and style preferences. You can choose from a fluoroelastomer strap that mimics fabric or opt for the perforated leather band for a more refined look.

What navigation features does the Polar Grit X Pro offer?

The Polar Grit X Pro is equipped with advanced navigation features such as built-in GPS, turn-by-turn navigation in partnership with Komoot, ‘back to start’ route guidance, and a breadcrumb trail feature for comprehensive route tracking.

Does the Polar Grit X Pro have smartwatch-like features such as music control and mobile payments?

While the Polar Grit X Pro does not support mobile payments, it boasts smartwatch features such as music controls and smartphone notifications. The main focus of the watch remains on performance tracking and fitness features suited to outdoor multisport activities.

How accurate is the heart rate monitoring on the Polar Grit X Pro?

The Grit X Pro uses Polar’s Precision Prime sensor technology for accurate and reliable heart rate monitoring. This ensures that athletes can trust the data recorded during their training sessions for analysis and improvement.

What is Training Load Pro™, and how does it work on the Polar Grit X Pro?

Training Load Pro™ is a feature on the Polar Grit X Pro that helps athletes track the strain of their training sessions and how it affects their body. It provides insights into cardiovascular load, muscle load, and perceived load, giving a holistic view of training’s impact on performance and recovery.

Can the Polar Grit X Pro be used for tracking sleep?

Yes, the Polar Grit X Pro includes sleep-tracking functionalities that monitor your sleep patterns and quality, aiding in the assessment of your overall recovery pro and readiness for training.

What’s the battery life like on the Polar Grit X Pro, and how does it perform during long outdoor activities?
