The 3-Chart Stack I Use For Every Deal

1. Natal Overlay (Chemistry)

I start with a side-by-side list of the “Big 6” planets for each founder. The goal is spotting complementary elements. Earth-heavy CFO meets Fire-heavy CMO? That works—stability plus ignition. Two Mars-conjunct-Pluto CEOs? Unless you sell boxing gloves, pass.

Red flags I never ignore:

  • Sun square Sun (core vision clash)
  • Moon opposite Moon (emotional foreign languages)
  • Mars conjunct Saturn in synastry (one person inevitably blocks the other)

2. Composite Chart (The Baby)

Think of the composite as the “birth chart” of your venture. I once advised a SaaS team whose composite Sun sat on royal star Regulus. They hit $10 M ARR in 18 months—then composite Mars squared Saturn and the CTO quit on launch day. The chart showed it two years earlier.

Pay special attention to:

  • Composite Mid-heaven sign—this is your brand’s public face
  • Composite 8th-house planets—how you handle joint money
  • Solar arc directions to composite Sun—roughly every 7 years the business “changes clothes”

3. Transit & Progression Radar (Timing)

You can have great chemistry, but if you ink the paperwork minutes before Mercury stations retrograde, expect revisions. I use a one-year transit accordion: Jupiter for growth windows, Saturn for structure sprints, Uranus for pivot alerts.

Common Questions I Get From Founders

Does one tough aspect doom the venture?
No. A single square is a tension point, not a death sentence. Stack enough supportive trines and sextiles and you can absorb the friction.

What if I don’t have my exact birth time?
Use a sunrise chart. You’ll lose house precision but still catch major aspects; Mercury-Venus-Mars still talk.

Can we fix bad synastry with contracts?
Lawyers mitigate risk; they don’t delete energetics. Spell out decision rights, but also schedule monthly “reset” dinners timed to the waxing Moon—it works.

How often should we revisit the composite chart?
Annually on the business birthday. Track solar-arc hits to the composite Sun; they mark identity upgrades (re-brand, pivot, fundraise).

Is business astrology scientifically proven?
Not in the double-blind sense. It’s pattern recognition—like checking a weather radar before you host an outdoor event. Smart founders use every data slice they can get.
