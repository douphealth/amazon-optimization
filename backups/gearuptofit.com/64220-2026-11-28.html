What are the most important features for an interval training watch in 2026?

Crucial features include advanced optical heart rate sensors for real-time accuracy, pre-loaded interval timers (like HIIT or Tabata modes), robust GPS for tracking pace/distance, and long battery life for multi-session tracking. Fast data syncing and durable, lightweight designs are also key.

Which sports watches are best for creating custom interval workouts?

The Garmin Forerunner 965 and Coros Pace 3 excel here. Their companion apps allow you to easily build complex, multi-step intervals based on time, distance, or heart rate zones, then sync them directly to the watch for guided, hands-free training sessions.

How important is GPS accuracy for interval training on a track?

It’s vital for measuring precise pace and distance per interval. Watches like the Garmin Forerunner 255/955 use dual-band GPS for superior accuracy on tracks, eliminating signal drift. This ensures your 400m repeat data is reliable for tracking performance progress over time.

Can I use a sports watch for pool-based interval swimming?

Yes. Top picks like the Garmin Fenix 7 Pro and Apple Watch Ultra 2 feature advanced swim tracking. They automatically detect stroke type, count laps, and record rest intervals between sets. Look for watches with waterproof ratings of 5 ATM or higher for pool use.

What watch offers the best balance of interval features and battery life?

The Coros Pace 3 is a standout, offering extensive custom interval programming, excellent GPS accuracy, and a battery life exceeding one week in smartwatch mode. It delivers premium interval functionality without the frequent charging required by some OLED-screen competitors.

Do these watches provide recovery metrics after hard interval sessions?

Yes. High-end models from Garmin, Polar, and Coros include advanced recovery tools. These analyze workout load, stress, and sleep to provide metrics like Training Readiness or Body Battery, advising optimal rest time before your next high-intensity interval workout.
