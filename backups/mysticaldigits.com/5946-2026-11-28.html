How to Calculate Both Systems in Under 5 Minutes

Life Path Digit (Western Pythagorean)

  1. Write your birth date: July 20, 1989 ➜ 07 / 20 / 1989
  2. Reduce each unit separately:
    • Month: 0 + 7 = 7
    • Day: 2 + 0 = 2
    • Year: 1 + 9 + 8 + 9 = 27 ➜ 2 + 7 = 9
  3. Add the reduced numbers: 7 + 2 + 9 = 18 ➜ 1 + 8 = 9. Life path 9.
