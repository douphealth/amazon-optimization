Carb Loading Strategy for 13.1 Miles

Forget the 90’s pasta binge. For 13.1, 36-48 h at 10 g kg⁻¹ tops off glycogen without GI distress. I add 3 L water + 3 g sodium daily so the carbs actually stay in my muscles, not the toilet. White rice, rice noodles, sourdough, rice cakes, and peeled sweet potato are my go-to low fiber foods before half marathon day.

Best Pre-Run Breakfast Half Marathon

I eat 3 h pre-gun: 1 g kg⁻¹ carbs, 0.3 g kg⁻¹ protein, <5 g fat, <2 g fiber. That’s 70 kg me = 70 g carbs. My favorite combo:

2 slices white sourdough (40 g carbs) + 2 Tbsp honey (30 g) + weak coffee (100 mg caffeine). I sip 500 mL sports drink while pinning my bib.

Caffeine strategy for half marathon race day: 3-6 mg kg⁻¹ 45 min pre-start improves time by 2-3 % without jitters. I skip if the forecast is >75 °F—thermoregulation beats the tiny boost.

Half Marathon Fueling During Race

Runs over 75 min benefit from 30-60 g carbs h⁻¹. I take my first gel at mile 5, second at mile 9. Gulp 150 mL water at each aid station to dilute stomach osmolality. My energy gels timing half marathon rule: “5 & 9, you’ll feel fine.”

Half Marathon Nutrition Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Fiber overload race-eve: Kale salad at 7 pm = 7 porta-potty stops at mile 9.
  2. Trying new gels on course: Stick to what you trained with.
  3. Over-hydrating: Hyponatremia kills more runners than dehydration.
  4. Skipping caffeine: Legal 2-3 % speed boost left on the table.
  5. Post-race beer before carbs: Alcohol blunts glycogen resynthesis 25 %.

FAQ

What nutrition do I need for a half marathon?

Carbs dominate: 30-60 g h⁻¹ during, 1.2 g kg⁻¹ post, plus 16-20 oz fluid with 200-300 mg sodium h⁻¹.

What are the best carbs for a half marathon?

Low-fiber, high-GI: white rice, rice cakes, sourdough, bananas, and maltodextrin gels.

How many carbs per day for half marathon training?

5 g kg⁻¹ on normal days, 7-10 g kg⁻¹ during carb-load.

How much protein do I need?

1.2-1.4 g kg⁻¹ daily; 0.3 g kg⁻¹ within 15 min post-finish.

Can I run a half marathon without gels?

Yes, if you finish under 75 min and had a solid pre-race breakfast. Over 75 min, gels improve performance.
