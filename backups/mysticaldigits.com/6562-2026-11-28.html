🔥 What Is Your Life Path Number? (The 2026 Calculation)

The Life Path Number is the sum of your complete birth date, reduced to a single digit or Master Number. It represents your fixed traits, overall purpose, and the lessons you're here to learn. It is not the only number, but it is the most significant.

Calculating this manually is simple, but precision matters. I've seen countless errors in the "reduce at the end" method. The correct Pythagorean method reduces at each step.

Here is the exact formula for a birth date of July 25, 1990:

1

Step 1: Reduce the Month

Month: 7 (July). It's already a single digit.

2

Step 2: Reduce the Day

Day: 25 → 2 + 5 = 7.

3

Step 3: Reduce the Year

Year: 1990 → 1 + 9 + 9 + 0 = 19 → 1 + 9 = 10 → 1 + 0 = 1.

4

Step 4: Add & Reduce

Add: 7 (Month) + 7 (Day) + 1 (Year) = 15 → 1 + 5 = 6. Life Path Number is 6.

Warning: Do not reduce Master Numbers 11, 22, or 33 during the process. If you get 11/22/33 at any step, that is your number.

Yes. Your address vibrates. A house totaling 5 (14, 23, 32) is a hub of social activity. A house totaling 8 (17, 26, 35) is great for business owners. Ensure the vibration aligns with your goals.
