The Bagua Map

The Bagua Map is a tool used in Feng Shui for analyzing the energy flow within a space. It’s an octagonal grid that represents the eight cardinal directions and corresponding life areas. The Bagua map is placed over the floor plan of a house or room to determine where certain life areas are located and how they interact with each other.

Using the Bagua map, you can identify which life area corresponds to which part of your home or office. There are eight life areas represented on the map: wealth/prosperity, fame/reputation, relationships/love, family/ancestors, health/wellness, creativity/children, knowledge/self-cultivation, and career/life path.

By understanding how these different energy flows interact with each other within your space, you can adjust and enhance them to create more harmony and balance in your life.
