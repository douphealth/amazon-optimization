How to Calculate Your House or Apartment Number

The method depends on your address type. The key rule: use the number that corresponds most directly to where you sleep most nights, and use the same number consistently.

Street Address Only

For a standalone house, use the street number only. Add the digits and reduce:

  • 42 Maple Street → 4+2 = 6 → House number 6
  • 15 Oak Avenue → 1+5 = 6 → House number 6
  • 108 Willow Road → 1+0+8 = 9 → House number 9
  • 7 Pine Lane → 7 → House number 7

Apartment or Unit Number

For an apartment reading, use the unit number that identifies your own home. Building-only and combined readings are alternative conventions, not equivalent calculations:

  • Building number method: Use the street address of the building (e.g., Apartment 3 at 42 Maple → 42 → 4+2 = 6)
  • Full apartment number method: Use the apartment number itself (e.g., Apartment 14 → 1+4 = 5)
  • Combined method: Add both building and apartment numbers together (e.g., 42 + 14 = 56 → 5+6 = 11, a master number)

This guide recommends the unit number for the personal apartment reading. Calculate the building number separately if you want to compare interpretations; do not switch conventions midway through a calculation.

Letters, Hyphens, and Special Cases

  • Letter suffixes (e.g., 14B): Some numerologists convert the letter to a number using A=1 through I=9, J=1 through R=9, S=1 through Z=8, then add it to the street number. Others ignore letters. The letter conversion: A=1, B=2, C=3, D=4, E=5, F=6, G=7, H=8, I=9, J=1, K=2, L=3, M=4, N=5, O=6, P=7, Q=8, R=9, S=1, T=2, U=3, V=4, W=5, X=6, Y=7, Z=8.
  • Hyphenated addresses: Use the main street number, not the secondary number
  • Numbers with zeros: Zeros add nothing (e.g., 105 → 1+0+5 = 6)
  • Unit/Flat numbers: See apartment method above

What House Number Numerology Cannot Tell You

  • It is not a property investment strategy — do not choose or avoid a home based on its numerology number alone. Location, budget, safety, and practical factors matter more
  • It does not predict your luck — there is no evidence that house number numerology affects outcomes in any measurable way
  • It does not determine your relationships — who you live with and how you communicate matter far more than your address number
  • It is not a replacement for professional advice — for financial, legal, or safety concerns about property, consult the relevant professionals
  • It is a reflection tool — useful for thinking about what kind of energy you want in your home, not for making binding decisions

Practical Use: Energy You Want in Your Home

If you find house number numerology useful as a reflection exercise, consider: what kind of energy do I want in my living space? A couple seeking harmony might appreciate a 2-energy home. An artist might seek a 3. Someone building a business from home might consider a 4 or 8. Use the number as a conversation starter with yourself — not a rule.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I use the apartment number or the building number?

Use your apartment or unit number for the personal reading in this guide. For example, Flat 14 at 42 High Road gives 1 + 4 = 5. The building number gives a separate 4 + 2 = 6 reading. Different traditions use different conventions; do not confuse these results.

What about letters in my address, like 14B?

Some numerologists convert the letter using numerology's letter-to-number chart (A=1 through Z=8/J=1, K=2, etc.), then add it to the street number. Others simply ignore letters and use the number only. Both approaches are used in practice. If you prefer a simple method, ignore the letter. If you want to include it, use the standard numerology letter chart.

What does house number 8 really mean?

In numerology traditions, house number 8 is commonly associated with abundance, ambition, and material themes — business, finance, and manifestation. This does not mean living in an 8-home will make you wealthy. It means that if this theme resonates with you, you might feel particularly inspired to focus on practical, ambitious projects while at home. The number 8 does not predict financial outcomes.

Do zeros count in house number numerology?

Zeros add nothing in numerology. If your address is 105, adding the digits as 1+0+5 = 6 is the same result as adding 1+5 = 6. Some calculators include zeros in the sum, but the practical result is identical.

Should I choose my home based on numerology?

No. Numerology is a symbolic reflection tool, not a property selection strategy. Factors like budget, location, safety, commute, natural light, and lease terms are far more consequential than a house number. If you are already living in a home and want to explore what the number means to you, that is the appropriate use of this framework.
