1. Write a character who is both good and evil. This type of character can be very complex and interesting to readers. They may find themselves rooting for the character even though they know they’re doing bad things.
2. Write a character who is struggling with a difficult decision. This type of character can be relatable to readers, as we’ve all had to make difficult choices in our own lives.
3. Write a character who is different from you in some way. This type of character can help you to see the world from a different perspective.

Clear Prompt Instructions

When writing a prompt about Crafting Compelling Characters: Creating Depth and Relatability, it’s important to be clear and concise. Here are a few tips for writing a great prompt:

* Start with a strong hook. This will grab the reader’s attention and make them want to know more.
* Be specific about the type of character you’re looking for. What are their personality traits? What are their goals? What are their challenges?
* Give the reader enough information to start brainstorming. Don’t give away too much, but provide enough context so that the reader can come up with their own ideas.

Tips for Crafting a Compelling Story

In addition to writing a strong character, there are a few other things you can do to craft a compelling story:

* Develop a strong plot. The plot should be engaging and keep readers guessing.
* Create believable characters. Readers need to be able to relate to your characters and believe that they could exist in real life.
* Use vivid language. Use descriptive language to bring your story to life.
* Edit carefully. Make sure your story is free of errors before you share it with the world.
