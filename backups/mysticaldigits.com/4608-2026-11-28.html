How to Calculate Event Date Numbers

The process of reducing multi-digit numbers is key to getting the single-digit number used in numerology.

  1. Write out the date (e.g., December 25, 2024 = 12/25/2024)
  2. Reduce the month: 1 + 2 = 3
  3. Reduce the day: 2 + 5 = 7
  4. Reduce the year: 2 + 0 + 2 + 4 = 8
  5. Add all of the numbers: 3 + 7 + 8 = 18
  6. Reduce to single digit: 1 + 8 = 9

Therefore, December 25, 2024, carries the energy of a number 9.

Day of the Week Significance

Each day is associated with a specific planet and thus a specific energy.

  • Monday: Ruled by the moon. Associated with emotions, new beginnings, and intuition. Good for events that are emotional or creative.
  • Tuesday: Ruled by Mars. Associated with action, energy, and drive. Suitable for events that require dynamic energy.
  • Wednesday: Ruled by Mercury. Associated with communication, intellect, and travel. Ideal for networking or educational events.
  • Thursday: Ruled by Jupiter. Associated with expansion, abundance, and growth. Best suited for big business events.
  • Friday: Ruled by Venus. Associated with love, beauty, and harmony. Perfect for weddings and celebrations.
  • Saturday: Ruled by Saturn. Associated with structure, responsibility, and hard work. Suited for very serious events.
  • Sunday: Ruled by the Sun. Associated with creativity, self-expression, and joy. Best for celebrations and social gatherings.
