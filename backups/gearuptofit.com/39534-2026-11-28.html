What Are Macros & Why Calorie Counting Isn’t Enough

Macros (short for macronutrients) are protein, carbohydrates, and fat. A 500-calorie slice of pepperoni pizza and a 500-calorie salmon plate may be equal in energy, but their macronutrient distribution for fat loss is wildly different. Protein protects muscle, carbs fuel training, and fat keeps hormones humming. Ignore the split and you risk skinny-fat syndrome, binge attacks, and metabolic slowdown.

Key takeaway: calories set the budget, macros write the blueprint.

Step-by-Step: How to Calculate Your Macros for Weight Loss

1. Estimate Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)

Use the same formula physiologists published in the 2025 Journal of Strength & Conditioning:

TDEE = (10 × weight kg) + (6.25 × height cm) – (5 × age) + 5   ♂
TDEE = (10 × weight kg) + (6.25 × height cm) – (5 × age) – 161 ♀
Multiply by activity factor: 1.2 sedentary, 1.375 light, 1.55 moderate, 1.725 very active.

Need help? My BMR/TDEE calculator spits out the number in seconds.

2. Create a Deficit That Doesn’t Crash Hormones

Subtract 15–20 % for 1–1.5 lb loss per week. Dropping 2 lb weekly (≈ 1 000 kcal deficit) is possible, but only if you cycle calories—e.g., –1 200 kcal Mon-Thu, maintenance Fri-Sun. This prevents leptin from nosediving.

3. Set Protein First

Meta-analysis of 2025 shows 1.6–2.2 g per kg body weight preserves lean mass in a deficit. I round to 2 g for simplicity. A 70 kg woman needs 140 g protein = 560 kcal.

4. Distribute Remaining Calories Between Carbs & Fat

There’s no single “magic” macro split for cutting, but these ranges work:

  • Protein 25–35 %
  • Fat 20–30 %
  • Carbs 35–45 %

Endomorph? Shift fat up, carbs down. Vegan athlete? Keep carbs higher. The table below shows three evidence-based templates.

Adjusting Macros When Weight Loss Stalls

Plateaus happen at week 3, 6, and 12. First, confirm you’re tracking alcohol correctly (7 kcal g). Next, drop 5 g fat or 15 g carbs (≈ 50 kcal) and reassess in 10 days. If waist measurements still freeze, implement a refeed day: +400 kcal from carbs every 10–14 days to restore leptin.

Common Mistakes That Break the Macro Calculator

  • Using cooked rice weight instead of dry (carbs double)
  • Ignoring fiber (net carbs = total – fiber)
  • Forgetting condiments (1 tbsp ketchup = 4 g sugar)
  • Overestimating activity level (desk job ≠ very active)

Visual Guide: Reading Nutrition Labels for Macros

Flip the package. Protein and carbs are listed in grams; fat too. Ignore “% daily value” and do your own math: grams × 4 for protein/carbs, × 9 for fat. Compare to your daily macro goals.

How to calculate your macros if you want to lose weight?

Find TDEE, subtract 15–20 %, set protein at 2 g/kg, then fill remaining calories with carbs/fat in a 35-45-25 split. Use the free spreadsheet above for auto-math.

What is the 50 30 20 rule for weight loss?

It’s a budgeting rule, not macros. For money: 50 % needs, 30 % wants, 20 % savings. For macros the closest is 50 % carbs, 30 % protein, 20 % fat—fine for maintenance, not optimal for fat loss.

How many calories should I eat a day to lose 2 lb a week?

≈ 1 000 kcal deficit daily. Most active women land near 1 400–1 600 kcal, men 1 700–1 900 kcal. Cycle calories to keep hormones happy.
