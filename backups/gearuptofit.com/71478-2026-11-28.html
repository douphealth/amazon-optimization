What is the “Big 3” powerlifting routine and how does this relate?

Do deadlifts actually improve my squat strength?

Absolutely, and the mechanism is clear. Deadlifts are a premier posterior chain developer, strengthening the erector spinae, glutes, and hamstrings. This creates a more stable “shelf” for the barbell during a squat and improves force transfer from the ground up.
