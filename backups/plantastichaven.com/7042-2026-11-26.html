Yellow leaves are your plant's SOS signal. But what's causing it? Here's how to diagnose and fix the problem.

Quick answer: Where the yellowing appears tells you the cause. Yellowing on the lowest leaves with damp soil is almost always overwatering or poor drainage. Yellowing on new leaves, or between the veins while the veins stay green, points to a nutrient or root-uptake problem. Uniformly pale leaves across the whole plant usually mean too much direct sun. One or two yellow leaves at the very bottom is normal ageing and needs no action.

Start here: the yellow-leaf decision tree

Work down this tree in order. The first branch that matches your plant is the one to act on.

  • Is the soil damp more than a few days after watering?
    • Yes — and the yellowing is on the lowest leaves → overwatering or a mix that will not drain. Check the roots before you change anything else.
    • Yes — and there are brown spots with yellow halos → a fungal leaf problem, usually from water sitting on foliage.
    • No — carry on down the tree.
  • Which leaves are yellow?
    • Only the oldest, lowest one or two → normal ageing. Remove the leaf and stop worrying.
    • The newest leaves → a nutrient or root-uptake problem, most often nitrogen or iron.
    • Yellow between the veins, veins still green → interveinal chlorosis; usually iron or magnesium availability.
    • Every leaf, evenly pale → too much direct sun, or a long-term light or feeding imbalance.
  • Are the leaf edges also brown and crisp?
    • Yes → dry air or watering that swings between bone-dry and soaked.
    • No → stay with the branch you matched above.

Symptom-by-symptom reference

🟡 Lower Leaves Turning Yellow

Cause: Overwatering (most common)

Fix: Let soil dry out completely. Check for root rot — remove plant from pot, trim any brown/mushy roots, repot in fresh dry soil.

🟡 Upper/New Leaves Yellow

Cause: Nutrient deficiency (usually nitrogen or iron)

Fix: Feed with balanced liquid fertilizer (10-10-10) every 2 weeks during growing season.

🟡 Yellow with Brown Tips

Cause: Low humidity or inconsistent watering

Fix: Group plants together, use a pebble tray with water, or move away from heating vents.

🟡 Yellow with Brown Spots

Cause: Fungal infection (from wet leaves)

Fix: Remove affected leaves. Water at soil level only. Improve air circulation.

🟡 Pale Yellow All Over

Cause: Too much direct sunlight

Fix: Move to bright indirect light. Use a sheer curtain to filter harsh sun.

🟡 Yellow Between Leaf Veins

Cause: Magnesium or iron deficiency

Fix: Add Epsom salt (1 tsp per gallon of water) or chelated iron supplement.

🟡 One or Two Yellow Leaves at Bottom

Cause: Natural aging (totally normal!)

Fix: Just remove the old leaf. Your plant is fine.

Rule of thumb: A few yellow leaves at the bottom = normal. Yellow leaves everywhere = time to investigate.
