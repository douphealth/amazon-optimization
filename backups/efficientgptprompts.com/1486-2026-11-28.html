Prompts about Persuasive Writing Topics for Teenagers

1. Write a persuasive essay on the topic of whether or not teenagers should be allowed to vote. This is a hotly debated topic with strong arguments on both sides. Encourage your students to do their research and come up with a well-reasoned argument.
2. Write a letter to the editor of your local newspaper about a current event that you’re passionate about. This is a great way for your students to share their voice and make a difference in their community. Encourage them to choose an issue that they’re truly passionate about and to write a letter that’s both informative and persuasive.
3. Create a blog post about a personal experience that has shaped your views on a particular issue. This is a great way for your students to share their stories and connect with others who share their experiences. Encourage them to be honest and vulnerable, and to write a post that’s both personal and persuasive.

Clear Prompt Instructions

When writing a persuasive essay, it’s important to have a clear thesis statement that states your main argument. Be sure to support your thesis with evidence from credible sources, and to use strong persuasive language.

When writing a letter to the editor, be sure to follow the guidelines of the publication you’re submitting to. Be sure to include a clear and concise title, and to introduce yourself and your qualifications. State your argument in the first paragraph, and then support it with evidence in the following paragraphs.

When writing a blog post, be sure to choose a topic that you’re passionate about and that you know something about. Write an engaging introduction that will draw your readers in, and then support your argument with evidence and personal anecdotes.

Examples and Inspiration

Here are a few examples of popular books, movies, or TV shows that involve persuasive writing:

* The Great Gatsby by F. Scott Fitzgerald: This classic novel tells the story of Jay Gatsby, a wealthy man who throws lavish parties in an attempt to win back the love of his life. The novel explores the themes of class, wealth, and the American Dream.

These are just a few examples of popular stories that involve persuasive writing. There are many other great books, movies, and TV shows that can inspire your students to write their own persuasive essays, letters to the editor, and blog posts.

Tips for Crafting a Compelling Story

Here are a few tips for crafting a compelling story that will persuade your readers:

* Start with a strong hook. Your hook should grab your readers’ attention and make them want to read more.
* Develop your characters. Your characters should be relatable and well-developed. They should have clear motivations and goals that your readers can understand and empathize with.
* Create a strong plot. Your plot should be engaging and keep your readers guessing. It should have a clear beginning, middle, and end.
* Use strong persuasive language. Your language should be clear, concise, and persuasive. Use evidence to support your claims and avoid using emotional appeals.
