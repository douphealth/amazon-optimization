❓ Garmin Enduro 3: Frequently Asked Questions (2026)

Is the Enduro 3 worth upgrading from the Enduro 2?

Yes, for serious ultrarunners. The combination of the AMOLED display (30% brighter), 70+ extra hours of GPS battery, and the intelligent SatIQ system provides a meaningful performance boost. The 90-gram total weight savings on your wrist over 100 miles is a tangible reduction in fatigue.

Can I use the Enduro 3 for triathlon?

It’s 10 ATM swim-proof and has open-water swim tracking, but it lacks a dedicated multisport/brick mode for seamless triathlon racing. Dedicated triathletes will be better served by the Garmin Fenix 8 Pro or the Garmin Forerunner 965, which are built for quick transitions.

Does solar charging really make a difference?

On a cloudless 14-hour run, the Power Glass solar ring added about 8% battery, or roughly 28 extra hours of GPS time. For a 200-mile race or a multi-day fastpacking trip, that’s incredibly valuable. For daily training, it’s a nice bonus that extends time between charges.

How accurate is the wrist heart rate above 180 BPM?

The 5th-gen Elevate sensor is excellent. In my tests during 5-minute VO2 max intervals, it was within 3 BPM of a Polar H10 chest strap. For tempo, steady-state, and recovery runs, it’s essentially spot-on. The 2025 Garmin Enduro 3 represents the pinnacle of optical HR accuracy for sports watches.

What major features are missing?

Garmin omitted three things to maximize battery and minimize weight: 1) Onboard music storage (Spotify/Apple Music), 2) LTE connectivity for calls/texts without a phone, and 3) A built-in LED flashlight (found on the Fenix 8X). For its target audience, these are acceptable trade-offs.
