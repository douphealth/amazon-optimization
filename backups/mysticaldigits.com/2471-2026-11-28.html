FAQ

Can two Life Path 5s last?

Yes, but you need external glue—shared business, overseas postings, or collaborative projects. Otherwise the energy doubles and dissipates.

What’s the safest Mercury retrograde hack for 5s?

Schedule major talks two weeks outside the retrograde window. If you must sign papers, add a clause allowing revision 30 days post-wedding.

Is a prenup “unromantic” for a 5?

Hardly. It clarifies exit doors, which paradoxically lowers flight anxiety and boosts commitment in my client files.

How do kids factor into 5 freedom?

Plan for logistics support—nanny share, nearby grandparents, or homeschool-on-the-road plans. Five parents thrive when the world stays class-room-sized.
