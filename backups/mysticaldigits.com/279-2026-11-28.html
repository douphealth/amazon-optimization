Shadow Side & How to Outsmart It

Freedom can morph into flakiness. The most common complaint I hear from partners of 5s: “They’re here, then poof—gone.” FOMO drives impulse spending, thrill-seeking, even addictive tendencies.

Three fixes that actually stick:

  1. Two-Week Rule: Say yes to plans outward, schedule inward. If you still crave the outing in 14 days, commit cash.
  2. Anchor Ritual: Pick one daily constant (cold shower, 10-page novel read, kettlebell swings). It trains the nervous system for happy stillness.
  3. Accountability Text: Tell a friend your return ETA before you travel. No ghosting.
