Calculate Your Core Number in 30 Seconds

Forget the textbook paragraphs—do this now:

  1. Write your full birth date: MM/DD/YYYY.
  2. Add each digit left to right. Example: 07/22/1989 → 0+7+2+2+1+9+8+9 = 38.
  3. Keep adding until one digit remains: 3+8 = 11, then 1+1 = 2.
  4. If you hit 11, 22, or 33, stop—those are master numbers with their own stones.

Done? Circle that final digit; it's the key to everything below.
