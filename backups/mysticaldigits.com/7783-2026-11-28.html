8 Attitude – The Mogul

People think you’re billing by the minute. Budget hack: mention one free resource you love; it humanizes you.

9 Attitude – The Sage

You get the “old soul” label at 25. Social-media tip: share one actionable cause, not ten, or you’ll overwhelm.

Master 11/2 – The Spotlight

Inspiration oozes out, but you scare yourself with expectations. Grounding ritual: 4-7-8 breathing before any speech.

Same math, different lens. Birth-day focuses on raw talent; attitude focuses only on the vibe you leak to strangers.

Q2: Can my attitude change over time?

Core number stays, but maturity lets you dial it up or down. Think volume knob, not station change.

Q3: My partner and I clash. Should we worry?

Read both attitude digits first. If you’re 1 & 2, expect a pace difference—hardly a deal-breaker once you name it.
