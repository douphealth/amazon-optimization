🔢 How to Calculate Your Life Path Number in 2026

The Life Path number is the most important number in your numerology chart. It is derived from your birth date and represents your life's journey, your primary spiritual gifts, and the challenges you're here to master. Think of it as your soul's blueprint.

To calculate it with precision:

1

Write Your Full Birth Date

Use MM/DD/YYYY format. For example: August 15, 1985 becomes 08/15/1985.

2

Reduce Each Component

Month: 0+8=8. Day: 1+5=6. Year: 1+9+8+5=23, then 2+3=5.

3

Add & Reduce to Single Digit

8+6+5=19; 1+9=10; 1+0=1. Your Life Path is 1. Exception: 11, 22, 33 are Master Numbers—do not reduce.
