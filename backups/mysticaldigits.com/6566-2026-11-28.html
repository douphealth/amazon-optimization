FAQ

Q: Can I change my numbers if I hate my career?
A: Core numbers never change, but names can be tweaked (married name, pen name) to shift the Destiny emphasis—useful for a gentle pivot, not an escape hatch.
Q: Are master numbers better paid?
A: Not inherently. 11/22/33 add influence but also pressure; metrics from Glassdoor 2024 show no significant salary bump until the individual builds a personal brand.
Q: Is Personal Year the same as Chinese Zodiac year?
A: Nope. Personal Year is based strictly on your birth month/day plus calendar year and runs birthday to birthday.
Q: What if my Life Path and Destiny clash?
A: That tension is career gold—look for hybrid roles. Example: 7 (research) + 3 (perform) = science YouTuber, data-storyteller, TED speaker.
