2. The Technology: What Makes the Cloudsurfer 2 Special

CloudTec Phase® Sequential Cushioning

Unlike traditional Clouds that compress simultaneously, CloudTec Phase® collapses from heel to toe sequentially. The result is a rolling, wave-like sensation that smooths out each stride and reduces the “braking” feel many runners experience at heel strike.

100% Helion™ Superfoam

The entire midsole is now molded from Helion™, On’s proprietary superfoam blend. Compared to the original Cloudsurfer, energy return is up an estimated 13%, while weight drops by around 14 grams per shoe.

Rocker Geometry

A pronounced forefoot rocker encourages natural forward momentum — ideal for mid-foot and heel strikers alike.

10. Frequently Asked Questions

Is the On Cloudsurfer 2 good for beginners?

Yes — the generous cushioning, stable platform, and smooth rocker make it beginner-friendly. However, beginners with wide feet may prefer HOKA Clifton 9 for a roomier toe box.

Can I use the Cloudsurfer 2 for a marathon?

Absolutely. Many runners use it as their marathon shoe of choice, particularly for paces slower than 7:30/mile. For sub-3-hour attempts, consider a carbon-plated racer.

Does the Cloudsurfer 2 run small or large?

It runs true to size. If you’re between sizes or prefer a generous toe box, size up ½.

Is it good for overpronators?

No. The Cloudsurfer 2 is a neutral shoe. Overpronators should consider the On Cloudflyer 4 or a dedicated stability shoe.

What’s the difference between Cloudsurfer 1 and Cloudsurfer 2?

The Cloudsurfer 2 uses a 100% Helion™ midsole (vs. a blended foam), features a redesigned upper with better lockdown, and has improved rocker geometry. It’s roughly 14 g lighter.
